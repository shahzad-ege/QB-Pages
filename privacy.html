Privacy Policy

App: EGE Weekly Report Automation

This application is a private, internal tool used solely to synchronize QuickBooks Online accounting data (transactions, vendors, chart of accounts) into an internal database for financial reporting purposes on behalf of EGE Holdings and its affiliated entities.

What data is accessed: Purchase, Bill, and BillPayment transaction records, including line-item descriptions, vendor names, amounts, and account categories.

How data is used: Solely to populate internal financial reporting tables. Data is not sold, shared, licensed, or disclosed to any third party for any purpose.

Data retention: Data is retained in a private, access- controlled internal database for as long as needed for financial reporting purposes.

Access revocation: The QuickBooks Online account holder may disconnect this application at any time via QuickBooks Online's connected apps settings, which immediately revokes this application's access.
